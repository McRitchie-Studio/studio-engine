- **Integrations registry: QuickBooks, Egnyte and Microsoft 365 connection definitions, and an admin page.** The engine now ships a connection definition for each (URLs, scopes, token behaviour, a one-call describe of the account; no reader). QuickBooks keeps its access token until 5 minutes before it expires or a 401, then refreshes once under the row lock and stores the rotated refresh token; it captures Intuit's `intuit_tid` header on every call (new generic `trace_header:` provider option: logged per call, `provider.last_trace_id`, and on every raised error as `trace_id` and in its message). A host enables one in an initializer with `Studio.enable_integration :quickbooks, env: { client_id: "...", client_secret: "..." }`, naming its own env vars, and mounts `Studio.draw_integrations_routes(self)`: `/admin/integrations` lists each status and Connect, and `/admin/integrations/<key>` shows its connections, Disconnect, the redirect URI to register (`/admin/integrations/<key>/callback`) and the host's own panel (`panel:`). Stand-ins walk a connect end to end in development and test. See `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md`.
