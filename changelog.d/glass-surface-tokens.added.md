- **The glass surfaces take the app's tint: `--glass-surface-tint` and `--glass-surface-shadow-tint`.** Under `surface_style = :glass`, the light theme's card hairline (`--surface-glass-line`), the soft lights' shade (`--lit-soft-bottom`, `--lit-soft-pressed`, `--lit-badge`, `--lit-input`) and the card shadow (`--surface-glass-shadow`) were the hub's violet for every app. They now read two root tokens, as `R G B`. The defaults are the old values, so an app that sets none looks as it did. An app sets them in its own stylesheet, unlayered, never in `@layer components` (the engine's default wins there and nothing reports it). The dark theme reads neither token. `docs/NEW_APP_SETUP.md` § 9 has the example. An app that already overrides the `--surface-glass-*` or `--lit-*` variables directly keeps working.
