- **`studio/_carousel` takes `style: :cascade`, a 3D stacked carousel.** The open slide sits in front at full size; the slides either side step back behind it, each step smaller, deeper and dimmer, turned a little toward the centre. `visible: 5` (the default) shows two each side, `visible: 3` one, and a region under 36rem wide shows one whatever it asks. Arrows, dots, keys, swipe or drag, a click on a side slide and keyboard focus bring a slide to the front; `loop:`, `start:`, `autoplay:` and `caption:` work as on the spotlight, and a looping cascade needs no clones. Reduced motion lays it flat as a spotlight row. `:standard` and `:spotlight` render byte for byte as before. A host adopts it by changing `style: :spotlight` to `style: :cascade`; `visible:` on any other style raises.
