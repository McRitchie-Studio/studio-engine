- **Glass is the standard for a new app.** Alex McRitchie ruled on 2026-11-09 that glass surfaces and the glass sidebar are the standard. The new-app initializer in `docs/NEW_APP_SETUP.md` now sets `config.surface_style = :glass` and `config.sidebar_style = :glass`, and its layout writes `data-surface-style` on `<html>`. The engine default stays `:default`, so an existing app does not change when it bumps the gem. Nothing to do unless you start a new app.
- **The glass sidebar takes the app's colours from root tokens.** It hard-coded the hub's violet, so every app that adopted it restated the shell's `--glass-*` variables. The shell now reads `--glass-sidebar-fill` and `--glass-sidebar-tint` (`R G B`) and `--glass-sidebar-ink` and `--glass-sidebar-soft-ink` from `:root` and `.dark`, and their defaults are the old violet values, so the hub and every app that sets none paint exactly as before. An app that overrides the `--glass-*` variables on the shell keeps working; it can move those values to the four tokens (example in `docs/NEW_APP_SETUP.md` § 9).
