- **Modals load on demand (`Studio.modals`).** Register a modal in an initializer (`Studio.modals.register :contest_rules, partial: "modals/contest_rules"`, with `auth: :user`, `prefetch: true` and `locals:` as options) and delete its template from the layout. The shared host fetches it from `GET /_studio/modals/:id` on its first open, shows the processing card when the fetch is slow and the error card when it fails, and keeps it for the page's lifetime, Turbo visits included. Call sites do not change. An app that registers nothing renders the host as before. See the README, "Modals on demand".
