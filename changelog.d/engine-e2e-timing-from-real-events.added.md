- **Shared reveal waits for page transition specs.** `e2e_helpers.js` adds `watchReveal`, `revealed`, `settled`, `arrivalSettled`, `frames`, `sessionLog` and `logOnRed`, moved from the engine's full-load spec. They wait on the new page's `pagereveal` and its view transition, never on a pause, and a red prints and attaches the event log. A host's own transition spec can drop its copies and require these from `node_modules/.studio-engine/e2e_helpers.js`.
