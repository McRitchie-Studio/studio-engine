- **`.lit-tile` and `.lit-tile-soft`.** Under `surface_style = :glass`, an app's own call-to-action tile that is not a `.btn` takes the buttons' light: a top light, a bottom shade and a contact shadow (`.lit-tile`), or the outline button's softer light (`.lit-tile-soft`), with a pressed state. Shadows only, so the box, the corners and the fill stay the app's. Without glass on the root, both classes do nothing.
