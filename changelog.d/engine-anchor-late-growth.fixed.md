- **A tab's jump stays on its section when a lazy frame above it loads after the jump.** The body has scroll anchoring off (`studio/_head`), so a lazy frame that the smooth scroll passed and that landed after the scroll ended (the style guide's Loaders feed, 88px above Modals) pushed the section down under the pinned row, and the tab above it took the highlight. For 3 seconds after the end (`HOLD_MS`), `studio/in_page_links` now moves the page with the target, as scroll anchoring would, until the reader's first wheel, touch, key or press, or any scroll it did not make. A `scrollend` that comes after the jump aims again but before that aim's first scroll (Chrome sends the replaced scroll's end) no longer ends the jump short. Where the browser fires `scrollend`, a scroll that `scrollend` never follows (a page that shortens, and the browser pulls the scroll back) now ends the jump 400 ms after its last scroll event; before, the jump stayed open and the tab stayed highlighted. Nothing for a consumer to do.
